CueWoven

Search your history from a vague memory.

Chrome Web Store · Edge beta

Remember a video or article but not its name? Describe what you remember, like something that was said, something you saw, or roughly when, and CueWoven finds it.

Your library is saved and searched on your computer with local AI models.

  • No account

Part Finder

Find the next part of a YouTube series.

Coming soon · Microsoft Edge

Watching Part 3 and can't find Part 4? Part Finder shows the next part of the series you're watching, with one click on the video page.

Full timeline finds every part in order, including ones you missed, and works with Shorts, numbered episodes and series that only say "update".

  • No account
  • Free to use
  • Official YouTube API
