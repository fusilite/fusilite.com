# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages (CNAME fusilite.com). No framework or build step for the home page. Privacy pages are generated by `build-privacy.mjs` from each extension repo's `docs/PRIVACY.md`.

## Users

- People deciding whether to install one of the tools: they arrive from a store listing, a link or a search, and want to know quickly what the tool does, whether it is trustworthy, and where to get it.
- Store reviewers and careful users checking the privacy policy and support contact. The privacy URLs are linked from the store listings and must not move: `/cuewoven/privacy/` and `/partfinder/privacy/`.

## Product Purpose

fusilite.com is the home of FusiLite Labs: one person's small, private browser extensions. It explains each tool, shows it, links to its privacy policy and source where public, and gives one support address. Success: a visitor understands a tool in seconds and trusts it enough to install it; a reviewer finds the policy and contact without searching.

## Positioning

Made by one developer, not a company. Tools do one job, need no account, and do not track. CueWoven searches your history on your own computer with local models; Part Finder finds the next part of a YouTube series. The maker's own voice ("I build…") is the honest differentiator.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- CueWoven: on the Chrome Web Store (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cuewoven-search-your-hist/pmckegghofkmajmdnlcnallgbgpgonij); Microsoft Edge beta. Describe a vague memory of a video or article and it finds it; library saved and searched locally with local AI models. Source: https://github.com/eric-chenCS/CueWoven
- Part Finder: Microsoft Edge, coming soon (store submission pending). Finds the next part of the YouTube series you are watching; Full timeline lists every part in order. Uses YouTube API Services (official YouTube Data API) via a free shared server or the user's own key. Source repo is private.
- CueWoven has a Chrome Web Store link (above). Part Finder has no store link yet; do not invent one. When its listing goes live, swap the status for the link.
- Support: support@fusilite.com (forwards to the owner).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FusiLite Labs. Domain fusilite.com.
- Existing tagline: "Small, private browser tools with no accounts and no tracking."
- Voice: first person, one maker; plain words, short sentences, no hype.
- Each product keeps its own icon and palette (Part Finder: evening plum + amber).

## Product Principles

1. Say what each tool does in one sentence a stranger understands.
2. Show the real product, not decoration.
3. Privacy claims stay specific and true; link the full policy.
4. One person's voice; no corporate "we" or marketing superlatives.
5. Nothing on the page tracks visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark; readable at phone width; works without JavaScript.
