Scope: home page (index.html). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: people deciding whether to install a FusiLite tool; reviewers checking privacy and contact.
Action: understand each tool in seconds, then install (or read privacy / report a problem). Proof: real screenshots, plain privacy facts.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS, no JS required, no tracking, privacy URLs unchanged, no invented store links or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one sticker sheet; each tool is a die-cut sticker you could peel off and use. Refuses the centered hero over a row of equal rounded feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Sunny marigold backing sheet owning the whole page (deep indigo sheet in dark mode), ink in deep indigo; stickers are vinyl in each product's own colours with a thick white die-cut border, kiss-cut dashed outlines printed on the sheet, small rectangular label stickers for facts. Chunky groovy display lettering, hyperlegible body.
STORY: The visitor reads an optimistic line from one maker, sees two tools as stickers, peels one to its section with the real screenshot and plain facts, and installs or reads the policy.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds: headline "Small tools for better days." huge, "One at a time." below it in yellow, one first-person sentence. Right: the two product stickers at real sticker scale, tilted, overlapping slightly, each with icon, name, one-line job and status tag; label stickers "No accounts" "No tracking" under the copy. Primary action: each sticker links to its tool's section.
